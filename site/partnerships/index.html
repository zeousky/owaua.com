E.R.G.O

the other bot · Discord

owaua hangs out. E.R.G.O does chores: a wallet, a shop, music, mods, fishing, news, and a little AI with its own memory. The long name is Executive Response & Governance Operator.

Type !menu in Discord for the live list. Some of it is staff-only. It isn’t us, and it doesn’t share memory with owaua. If this page and the bot ever disagree, believe the bot.
