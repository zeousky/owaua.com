privacy

what it remembers

Last updated October 24, 2026. Official bot only, client id 1442127404607737999. Using it also means the terms.

what it keeps

  • your chatMessages you send it, and its replies, for that server, channel, and you. Not other people’s chats, and not a log of the room.
  • notes and settingsNotes you save, your persona, and your language. History lasts up to 180 days or 1,000 exchanges. Notes stay until you delete them.
  • files and mediaThings you attach go to xAI for that request. A saved document stays there until you remove it. Picture and video jobs are recorded so the daily limits hold.

who else sees it

Discord already has the message. To answer, owaua sends the prompt, useful context, and attachments to xAI. It asks xAI not to store text replies. That is not a promise about every log they keep. Music sends a song name or SoundCloud link to SoundCloud. A voice chat sends only the starter’s microphone. I don’t sell any of this.

deleting it

/reset clears your history and notes here. It does not clear your persona, language, saved documents, or media jobs. Use /knowledge clear for documents. Leaving a server does not wipe what’s already stored. Email ckazros@owaua.com with your Discord user id if you need more deleted. Don’t send passwords.

The site is static and doesn’t use a tracking tag. Write me at that email, Discord ckazros, or the Discord. You need to be 13 or older. The default voice swears. There is no adult mode.
