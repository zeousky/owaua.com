the agreement

Last updated October 24, 2026. If you invite owaua, talk to it, or keep it in a server, you agree to this.

01

what this covers

This is the agreement for the official owaua Discord bot, the app you invite from this site, client id 1442127404607737999. The website and the owaua Discord server sit with the same person.

Discord has its own terms, and you have to follow those too. If you run a copy of the code yourself, you are the operator of that copy. These terms are for the official bot.

02

who can use it

You need a Discord account, and you need to be old enough for Discord. That means 13 or older. Please don’t use the bot if you are under 13.

If you add it to a server, you are saying you have permission to do that, and that people in the server may be talking to a bot that stores recent chat.

03

what the bot is

owaua is a Discord bot you can chat with, ask to look things up, or use for voice, documents, images, videos, and music. Ping it, reply to it, use /ask or .ask, or send a DM. Your voice and language are personal. Memories are separate for each server, channel, and person. Music uses .music with a song name or a SoundCloud link. You review the prompt before a paid image or video starts.

It isn’t a person, and it isn’t a moderator. It can help, and it can be wrong. Don’t treat what it says as advice, and don’t hand it a decision that matters.

04

talking to it

Messages you send it, and its replies, can be stored locally. Relevant history, notes, and attachments can go to xAI so it can answer. History lasts up to 180 days and 1,000 exchanges in that chat. Notes last until you delete them. /memory pause stops memory here without deleting it. /reset or /memory clear erases your history and notes here. That does not remove your persona, your language, or a saved document.

Please don’t send passwords, tokens, or anything you wouldn’t want saved.

There’s about a five-second pause between your requests, and only a few can run at once. Each person gets two images and one video a day. Each server shares five images and three videos. Those reset at midnight in Budapest. Hitting a limit is not a ban. Trying to break it, spamming a channel, or burning the API on junk is.

More on storage is in the privacy policy.

05

please don’t burn the API

Replies and generated media cost real money. owaua is for conversations and things you actually want. It is not a benchmark, and it is not a toy for burning tokens.

Using it to waste that money is abuse. “I was just testing” doesn’t change that.

That includes looping it, farming it, pinging it for nothing, dumping huge pastes, encode-and-decode junk, jailbreak marathons, making it generate walls of filler, running scripts or extra accounts at it, and turning on expensive modes then spamming search, tools, or long thinking on garbage.

I can ignore you, wipe memory, pull the bot from the server, and stop answering, with no warning. A rate limit is not permission to keep doing it.

06

if you run a server

If you own or moderate a server with owaua in it, you’re responsible for how it’s used there. Let people know their requests can be stored and sent to the providers that answer them. Language and persona belong to each person. Memory controls affect that person’s own chat. There is no kick, ban, or other moderation command.

Please don’t use the bot to harass people, raid, spam, or dodge Discord’s rules.

07

please don’t

Don’t use owaua to:

break the rules

The law, Discord’s terms, or these ones.

waste the API

No looping, farming, junk prompts, scripts, or extra accounts to burn money or dodge the limits. I’ll stop answering, with no warning.

unsafe content

Do not use the bot to request or distribute illegal, abusive, or otherwise unsafe content.

attack the service

No scraping, flooding, or trying to knock the host or other users over.

impersonate

Don’t pretend to be owaua, the operator, or someone else.

dump other people’s data

Don’t feed it private stuff they did not choose to share with a bot.

steal media

Don’t ask it to fetch or share copyrighted audio or files you do not have the right to use.

ask for real harm

Malware, exploits, or instructions for hurting people or breaking systems in the real world.

I can ignore messages, reset memory, pull the bot from a server, or stop answering you.

08

what it says

Replies are generated. They can be biased, outdated, or just false. Please don’t treat them as medical, legal, financial, or crisis help. If you need a human, talk to a human.

The bot has safety checks. They can miss something, or block a harmless request. They are not a substitute for a person, or for emergency services.

You are responsible for what you do with an answer. If you paste it somewhere, that is on you.

I can change features, limits, models, and availability. I can turn the official bot off.
