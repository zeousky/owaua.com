Slash commands, or a dot: .ask. Private answers come by DM.

01

what it does

you start it

Mention it, reply to it, or DM it. Otherwise it stays quiet.

it remembers you

Your chat in that channel is yours. Notes stay until you delete them.

your voice

Casual, and it swears, unless you change it with /persona. .language picks your replies.

more than chat

Search, read a file, make a picture or short video after you say yes, talk in voice, or play a SoundCloud song.

02

commands

Every slash command also works with a dot.

/ask

Ask. Attach a picture, file, recording, or video if you want.

/persona

How it talks, just for you. .language picks the language.

/memory

Your notes and history here. /reset wipes them.

.music

A song name or SoundCloud link.

/voice

A voice chat. /speak and /transcribe turn text and audio into each other.

03

limits

5s

a short pause

About five seconds between requests. Only a few chats at once.

2

pictures and video

Two images and one video a day for you. A server shares five and three. Midnight in Budapest. You confirm first.

10m

music and voice

One player per server. SoundCloud, under an hour. Voice chats last up to ten minutes.

It can be wrong. If it matters, check it.

04

a few answers

Does it reply to every message?

No. Ping it, reply to it, or DM it.

Can I trust every reply?

No. It can sound sure and still be wrong.
