a discord bot you can talk to

The site is small. The bot is the part that matters.

the bot that hangs out in your server

It stays quiet until you talk to it. Then it can actually help.

Mention it, reply to it, or just DM it. It remembers your conversation in that channel, not everyone else’s. It can change how it sounds, answer in your language, look something up, make a picture or a short video, talk with you in voice, or put a song on.

come sit in the Discord invite owaua

Slash commands work. So does a dot, like .ask what time is it. If a command is private, the answer comes to your DMs, so leave those open. The message you typed in the server stays visible there.

01

what it does

Talk to it the way you’d talk to someone in the server. The voice and the language are yours, not the room’s.

it waits until you talk to it

It doesn’t jump into every message. Ping it, reply to something it said, or send a DM. A mention, /ask, or .ask all work. It leaves other bots alone, and it doesn’t keep a running log of everyone else in the channel.

it remembers your conversation

In that channel, your chat with it stays yours. Recent messages are right there. Older ones it can look back through, for about six months. If you tell it something worth keeping, it can save a note until you delete it. The person next to you has their own memory. /memory view shows yours, just to you.

it can sound like you want

Out of the box it talks like a friend. Casual, and it swears. If that’s not the mood, change it: pirate, robot, wizard, detective, teacher, or a short description of your own. That choice follows you. It doesn’t change the bot for everyone else.

your language, not the whole server’s

Try .language Hungarian. Replies to you use that language, including in other servers, DMs, and the next voice chat. .language auto goes back to matching how you wrote.

a song, if you want one

Hop into voice and try .music with a song name, or paste a SoundCloud link. More requests wait in line. Pause, resume, skip, or stop from that same channel. No playlists, livestreams, or YouTube links.

it can look things up

A small question can get a small answer. A bigger one can get a real explanation. It can search the web and X, look at a picture, read a PDF or a code file, and hand back a file or a simple chart.

pictures, video, and an actual voice

Ask for an image or a short video, or send one and say what to change. It shows you the prompt first, and nothing is generated until you say yes. /speak reads text out loud. /transcribe writes down a recording. /voice join is a real conversation in the voice channel.

02

how you talk to it

Every slash command also works with a dot. Your voice, language, memory, and saved files are yours. Music controls only work if you’re in the same voice channel.

/help or .help

The short version, when you forget.

/ask or .ask

Ask something. Attach a picture, a document, a recording, or a video you want changed. A mention, a reply, or a DM works too.

/persona set

Pick a character, or describe the voice you want. /persona reset brings the usual one back.

.language Hungarian

Replies to you, in that language. .language auto matches your message again.

.music song or SoundCloud link

Put a song on. Then queue, pause, resume, skip, or stop.

/memory view

See your notes. remember, forget, pause, and resume are the rest. Pause doesn’t delete anything.

/reset

Wipe your own history and notes in this channel. Your voice, language, and saved documents stay.

/knowledge add

Keep a document for later questions here. /ask library:true uses it.

/voice join

Talk in voice. It hears the person who started it. Everyone in the channel can hear the replies.

/speak and /transcribe

Text into audio, or a recording into text.

/jobs

Your recent pictures and videos here.

03

the limits

These exist so one person can’t run up the bill, and so I can leave it on. They’re limits. Using it still costs money on my side.

5s

give it a second

About a five-second pause between your requests, and only a few conversations going at once. If you’re already mid-chat, the next message waits.

2

pictures and videos are limited

Two images and one video a day for you. A server shares five images and three videos. Resets at midnight in Budapest. You confirm before any of it is spent.

20

music and voice stay small

One player per server, up to 20 songs waiting, SoundCloud tracks under an hour. A voice chat lasts up to ten minutes. Stop the music before you start talking.

It gets things wrong. If a reply matters, check it, or ask a person.

04

what it keeps

Your chats with it, notes you asked it to keep, and settings like your voice and language. That lives on the machine that runs the bot. When it answers, the useful parts go to xAI. A document you choose to save stays there until you delete it. None of this is sold, and there are no ads.

The longer version is the privacy policy. Inviting it also means the terms.

05

questions

Does it reply to every message?

No. Ping it, reply to it, use a command, or DM it. It stays quiet the rest of the time.

Why didn’t it answer?

Check that you actually pinged it, or used a command, then give it a moment. A moving bar means it’s working. If it’s still quiet, look at the limits above. It also ignores other bots.

Can I change how it sounds?

Yes. /persona set description:Talk like a pirate, or pick a built-in character. It’s only for you. /persona reset brings back the usual voice.

Does the music play?

Yes, for a SoundCloud track it can reach. Join voice, then .music and a song name or a track link. YouTube links and playlists won’t play.

Can I trust every reply?

No. It can be sure and still wrong. If something matters, check it yourself, or ask a person.

Where do I say something is broken?

The Discord server, or me: Discord ckazros · ckazros@owaua.com. Say what you tried and what happened. Please don’t paste tokens or private chats into a public channel.
