privacy

what it remembers

Last updated October 24, 2026. owaua remembers your conversations with it, useful notes, and your settings, so the next reply isn’t starting from nothing. It doesn’t keep a background log of the rest of the room.

01

what this is for

This is for the official owaua Discord bot, the app you invite from this site, client id 1442127404607737999. The website and the owaua Discord server sit with the same person.

Discord has its own privacy policy, and that still applies. If you run a copy of the code yourself, that copy is yours, and you are the one responsible for it. This page is only for the official bot.

Using the bot also means you agree to the terms.

02

who to write to

owaua is a privately run Discord app. Questions, deletion requests, and anything else about this page can go to ckazros@owaua.com, Discord ckazros, or the Discord server.

Include your Discord user id if you want a specific account looked up. Please don’t paste private chats into a public channel.

03

what it collects

Only what it needs to answer you, and to remember a little of the conversation so the next reply has something to go on.

who you are on Discord

Your user id, and the server and channel ids needed to keep conversations separate. DMs have their own conversation. Display names come with the message Discord already sent.

the conversation

Messages you send through mentions, replies, commands, or DMs, and the bot’s answers. Memory is separate for each server, channel, and person. It doesn’t save ordinary room chatter as shared context, and it ignores other bots.

attachments and saved documents

Images, documents, recordings, and videos you send can go to xAI for the thing you asked for. A temporary document is deleted after use when cleanup succeeds, with a one-hour provider expiry as a fallback. /knowledge add deliberately saves a document at xAI until you remove it. Local records keep the filename, size, and provider file id, not the document itself.

your settings and notes

Your persona and reply language follow your account. Notes and document libraries belong to your own conversation in that channel. Notes stay until you delete them.

media requests

Daily picture and video reservations, and job records, live on the host so the limits hold and a submitted video can be checked again after a restart. Ordinary chat has a short cooldown and a limit on how many conversations run at once.

mail you send me

If you email or DM me, that message is just a message. I keep what I need to answer it.

It doesn’t reply unless you talk to it. It also doesn’t keep a log of everyone else’s lines in the channel.

04

why it keeps any of this

So it can reply, remember what you told it, and keep the voice and language you asked for. So one person can’t wear it out. So it can play a song, speak, transcribe a recording, or make the picture or video you confirmed.

I don’t sell any of this. There are no ads. Your DMs are not a mailing list.

05

how memory works

On the machine that runs the bot, in a local database. Your history is keyed by server, channel, and you. Two people in the same channel have separate histories. The same person in two channels has two. By default, up to 16 recent exchanges can be used as context, within a 32,000-character budget. It can also look back through older exchanges from that same chat, and at notes you saved. Other people’s conversations are not included.

History is kept for up to 180 days, and up to 1,000 exchanges in that chat. Notes stay until you forget or clear them. Persona and language stay until you change them. Saved documents have their own delete commands.

Please don’t send passwords, tokens, or anything you wouldn’t want saved. If you already did, use /reset or /memory clear for this channel, remove any saved document separately, or write to me.

06

who else sees it

Discord already has the message. That’s their platform.

To write a reply, the bot sends your current prompt, relevant recalled context, your voice and language settings, and the attachments you asked it to use, to xAI. Text requests ask xAI not to store the response. That is not a promise about every log they keep. Web and X search, hosted code, speech, transcription, and any read-only lookup you turn on can send what that feature needs. Memory commands themselves don’t call a model.

/voice join sends only the starter’s microphone audio to xAI. Everyone in the channel can hear the replies. Those live sessions don’t read or save your chat history, and the raw audio isn’t written to the bot’s logs. A recording you attach to a question is different: the transcript becomes part of the conversation. A standalone /transcribe returns files without saving a chat.

Music sends the song name or SoundCloud link it needs to play the track. Making an image or video sends the prompt you confirmed. I’m not Discord, xAI, or SoundCloud. Their policies apply to what passes through them.

07

how long it stays

History stays until it falls off the exchange limit, expires after 180 days, you clear it, or I delete it for you. Notes, your voice, your language, and saved documents have their own delete controls. Picture and video job records stay until I delete them. The links themselves can expire sooner. Music queues disappear when the bot restarts.

Backups of the host can hold a copy of the SQLite file for as long as those backups exist. Treat that file like private chat, because it is.

08

your choices

If you don’t want a record, don’t talk to it. Leaving a server stops new server memory for you there. It does not, by itself, wipe what is already stored.

/memory pause stops reading and saving memory here until /memory resume. /reset or /memory clear deletes your history and notes in this channel. /memory forget removes one note. None of that deletes your persona, your language, or a saved document. Use /knowledge forget or /knowledge clear for documents. These commands also don’t erase media job records. Write to me if you need those removed.

If that isn’t enough, email ckazros@owaua.com with your Discord user id. Say whether you mean DMs, a server, or both. Messages still sitting in the Discord channel are Discord’s, not mine.

You can also stop using the bot, kick it from a server, or close your Discord account. Kicking it stops new collection in that server. It does not automatically purge the SQLite file.

09

this website

These pages are static. The menu and the contact link don’t set cookies, and the site doesn’t load a third-party tracking tag. The host still keeps ordinary request logs. Contact is just email.

10

age

You need a Discord account, and you need to be old enough for Discord. That means 13 or older. Please don’t use the bot if you are under 13.

The default voice is casual and it swears. There is no adult mode. Explicit sexual content stays off, whatever voice you pick.

11

the official bot vs running it yourself

Self-hosting means your SQLite file, your keys, your users, and your own privacy policy. The official bot is the one invited from this site.

I can change models, limits, and where the file lives. If that changes what gets collected in a way that matters, this page will say so.

12

if this page changes

If this page changes, the new text applies once it is up. The date at the top is when that happened. Keeping the bot after that is agreement to the new policy.

Questions go to ckazros@owaua.com, Discord ckazros, or the Discord server.
