what it does

  • you start itMention it, reply to it, or DM it. Otherwise it stays quiet.
  • it remembers youYour chat in that channel is yours, for about six months. Notes stay until you delete them. Other people have their own.
  • your voiceCasual, and it swears, unless you change that with /persona. .language picks the language of your replies.
  • more than chatIt can search, read a file, make a picture or short video after you say yes, talk in voice, or play a SoundCloud song.

limits

  • a short pauseAbout five seconds between requests. Only a few chats at once.
  • pictures and videoTwo images and one video a day for you. A server shares five and three. Resets at midnight in Budapest. You confirm first.
  • music and voiceOne player per server. SoundCloud, under an hour. Voice chats last up to ten minutes.

It can be wrong. If it matters, check it.

a few answers

Does it reply to every message?

No. Ping it, reply to it, or DM it.

Can I trust every reply?

No. It can sound sure and still be wrong.

Who runs it, and where do I report a problem?

I do. I’m ckazros. The Discord, or email. Don’t paste tokens or private chats in public. What it stores is in the privacy policy. Inviting it means the terms.
